REMARKABLE / PRIVACY POLICY

Your data and your wallet.

This policy explains the information that may be processed when you use REMARKABLE.

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Last updated: November 28, 2026

Information processed

Depending on the features you use, the site may process information you provide, token search terms, browser preferences, locally saved watchlist entries, wallet public addresses, and technical request information handled by the hosting or service providers.

Watchlist and browser storage

The token watchlist may be saved in your browser's local storage. It is not a cloud account feature and may be removed when you clear browser data or use another device.

Wallet information

A public wallet address and blockchain transactions are public by design. When you connect or use a launch provider, your address and transaction details may be shared with the wallet, blockchain, and third-party provider required to complete the action. Never disclose private keys or recovery phrases.

Third-party providers

Market data, launch services, wallet software, and blockchain explorers operate independently. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms. Requests to these providers may reveal technical information such as your IP address to them.

How information is used

Information may be used to provide requested features, display market information, preserve local preferences, protect the service, diagnose errors, and comply with legal obligations.

Retention and choices

Local watchlist data remains in your browser until you remove it or clear site storage. Blockchain records cannot generally be altered or deleted. You can disconnect your wallet, clear browser storage, or stop using third-party services.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated as the platform changes. The current version is published on this page.

Digital assets are risky. Verify information independently and never share your private key or recovery phrase.
