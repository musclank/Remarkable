A practical guide to exploring REMARKABLE, reviewing token information, and using launch tools.

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Last updated: November 28, 2026

What is REMARKABLE?

REMARKABLE is an onchain culture discovery interface. It helps visitors browse token projects, view market information from external providers, and access supported token-launch flows.

Explore tokens

  • Browse the Trending and New feeds.
  • Search by token name, symbol, or chain.
  • Filter supported networks and open a token card for details.
  • Save tokens to your local watchlist. Watchlist data is stored in this browser and may not sync between devices.

Review token details

Check the token name, symbol, chain, contract or mint address, market cap, volume, liquidity, and linked explorer or trading pair. Data may be delayed, incomplete, or unavailable.

Always verify the contract address on the relevant blockchain explorer and official project channels before interacting.

Launch token

Launch availability depends on the selected network and launch provider. Base launches use the supported Clanker flow. BNB Smart Chain launches use the supported Four.meme flow where available.

  1. Select the network and provider.
  2. Enter token details and upload an image if requested.
  3. Review all settings, fees, tax allocations, and recipient addresses.
  4. Approve the requested wallet signature or transaction only after checking its details.

A wallet signature may authenticate you; a blockchain transaction can spend assets and is generally irreversible.

Market data and providers

REMARKABLE may use Clanker, DexScreener, and network-specific launch providers. These are third-party services with their own availability, terms, and data quality. REMARKABLE does not control their endpoints or guarantee uninterrupted service.

Need help?

For support, use the official REMARKABLE community links shown on the website. Never share a seed phrase or private key with anyone.

Digital assets are risky. Verify information independently and never share your private key or recovery phrase.
