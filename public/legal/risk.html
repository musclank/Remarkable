REMARKABLE / RISK DISCLOSURE

Understand the risks.

Digital assets and decentralized token launches involve substantial risk, including the possibility of losing all funds.

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Last updated: November 28, 2026

Price and liquidity risk

Token prices can change rapidly. Markets may be thin, manipulated, or illiquid. You may be unable to sell at a desired price—or at all.

Smart contract and protocol risk

Smart contracts, launch platforms, bridges, wallets, and blockchain networks may contain bugs, be exploited, or fail. Transactions can be delayed, reverted, or permanently lost.

Token and issuer risk

Token creators may abandon a project, change plans, retain significant supply, or act maliciously. A token's presence in the feed is not an endorsement or a safety review.

Tax-token risk

Tax, reward, burn, liquidity, or recipient settings can affect trading behavior and token economics. Confirm percentages, recipients, minimum balances, and contract behavior with the launch provider before signing.

Fees and irreversible actions

Network gas, launch fees, swaps, and other charges may apply. Wrong-chain transactions, transfers to incorrect addresses, and signed transactions may not be recoverable.

Independent verification

Verify contract addresses, provider documentation, ownership and permissions, liquidity, token distribution, and all transaction details independently. Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.

Digital assets are risky. Verify information independently and never share your private key or recovery phrase.
