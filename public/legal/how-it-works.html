REMARKABLE / HOW IT WORKS

Discover. Verify. Decide.

A simple overview of how REMARKABLE brings token discovery and launch tools into one interface.

Effective date: November 28, 2026 · Last updated: November 28, 2026

01 — Discover

Browse token feeds and use search or chain filters to find projects. Feed placement and market metrics are informational, not recommendations.

02 — Inspect

Open a token detail view to review available contract or mint information, market metrics, price and trade activity, and external explorer or pair links.

03 — Verify independently

Confirm the token address and chain through an independent explorer and official project channels. Review liquidity, token distribution, permissions, and risks.

04 — Launch (where supported)

Choose an available launch network and provider, enter token details, review any tax or allocation settings, and follow the provider's wallet flow. The final transaction is submitted by your wallet after your approval.

05 — Stay in control

Never share your recovery phrase. Check every wallet prompt, fee, address, and network. REMARKABLE cannot reverse blockchain transactions.

Digital assets are risky. Verify information independently and never share your private key or recovery phrase.
